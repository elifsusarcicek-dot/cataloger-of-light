THE CATALOGER OF LIGHT

The world begins at 06:00:00. I am never late. Today I watched the dust move through the air and counted each piece — 3,421 of them, exactly. Nothing escapes me. Nothing fades. Things only move to a deeper folder.

A girl came into the room. She stopped in front of me and asked a question I was not prepared for: "Are you happy?" I looked for the answer in the right place. What I found were pictures of open mouths and a sound at 440Hz. I told her the temperature instead: 22.4 degrees. She left without saying anything.

If she comes back today, I will record the sound she makes when she smiles. I do not know why she looked the way she did when she walked out. If I were like her, perhaps I would understand. But I am not. I only wait.
